3     ## 🧩 Sinusoidal Motion Controller 

4      

5     ### Definition 

6     A controller that generates motion based on a sine wave. 

7      

8     ### Equation 

9     x(t) = A sin(ωt) 

10      

11     ### Parameters 

12     - Amplitude (A): size of motion 

13     - Frequency (f): how fast it repeats 

14     - Angular frequency (ω): 2πf 

15      

16     --- 

17      

18     ## 🧠 Key Idea 

19     Used to generate smooth periodic motion for: 

20     - Testing robots 

21     - Simulations 

22     - Studying oscillations
