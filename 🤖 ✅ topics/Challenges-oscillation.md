# Control Systems Notes 

## 🔁 Oscillation 

### Definition 

Repeated back-and-forth motion around equilibrium.     

### Parameters 

- Amplitude 

- Frequency 

- Period 

- Phase      
---       

## ⚡ Impedance Definition 

Resistance to motion when force is applied.    

### Equation 
F = Mẍ + Bẋ + Kx 
Where: 
- M = mass 
- B = damping 
- K = stiffness 

## 🧠 Key Insight 

- High stiffness → faster oscillations 
- High damping → motion stops quickly
